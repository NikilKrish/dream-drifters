# Enhanced B Theme

> Active Superdesign context. The original vanilla theme is preserved under `reference/archive/` and is not a production specification.

- Canvas: continuous deep ink with nearby tonal surfaces.
- Text: pearl, soft pearl and mist; never default black.
- Accent: sparse Dream Drifters cyan for primary actions, focus and active state only.
- Type: self-hosted Instrument Serif for display and Manrope Variable for body/UI.
- Geometry: 1360 px maximum width, 14 px panels, sharp editorial media and pill controls.
- Glass: navigation, proof, foreground Services panel, itinerary and Enquiry only.
- Motion: native scroll, restrained opacity/transform transitions, GSAP Services progression and package-to-itinerary continuity.
- Media: attached `discovery`, `operations` and `travellers` video families with responsive poster fallbacks.
- Breakpoints: mobile below 700 px, tablet 700–1099 px, full cinematic desktop at 1100 px and above.

Detailed tokens and accessibility requirements live in `../design-system.md` and the canonical root `design.md`.
